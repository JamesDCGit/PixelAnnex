How to play

🎨
Paint pixels
Click & drag on the map to paint. Each pixel costs 1 from your bucket, which refills over time.
⚔️
Conquer countries
Hold ~70–75% of a country's pixels to claim it. Smaller countries need a higher share.
🛡️
Fight back
When your homeland is over half lost, painting your own land grants bonus pixels — the more you've lost, the bigger the boost.
🎯
Encircle
Close a loop around enemy land to capture everything inside it at once, and earn a regen bonus.
🤝
Alliances
Team up via Discord — allied pixels count together toward conquest, and you can rally on shared targets.
👹
Monsters
UFOs, Krakens & Giant Lizards roam the map and wipe pixels in their path. Stay clear — or strike while they rampage.
